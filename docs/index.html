The timetables list times only at main stops; times at the stops in between are estimated by spacing them evenly by distance. Other routes are drawn on the map but can't be used for trips yet.

Walking times are straight-line distance with a detour allowance at about 4.8 km/h, so real walks may differ. Always check the official schedule before you travel.
