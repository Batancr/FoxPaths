Plan a whole day of stops around the bus

Walking times are straight-line distance with a detour allowance at about 4.8 km/h, so real walks may differ. Always check the official schedule before you travel.

Restaurants and cafés for "a place to eat on your way" come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors), loaded through the Overpass API.

Google Maps buttons open one leg at a time, because Google Maps doesn't support multi-stop trips by transit. Google shows its own live bus options, which may differ from this plan.
