Route 2 Chemong

Route description

Route 2 Chemong serves the shopping corridors along Chemong Road, on lower George Street and at Lansdowne Place. The route starts in the north end at Bata Library - Trent University, serves the downtown terminal and serves the west side of Lansdowne Place. It also serves the Reid Street, King George Street, and McKellar Street communities.

Plan your trip using Google Maps or the My Transit Ride GPS Tracker.
