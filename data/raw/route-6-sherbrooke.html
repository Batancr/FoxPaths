Route 6 Sherbrooke

Route description and planner

Route 6 Sherbrooke operates through the central core of the city linking Trent University West Bank, and the downtown terminal. It also serves the Cumberland Avenue, Hilliard Street, and  Sherbrooke Street communities.

Plan your trip using Google Maps or the My Transit Ride GPS Tracker.
